Tomato soup

Serves 4 45 minutes Vegetarian

A bright, simple soup for late-summer tomatoes. From chopping board to bowl in about 25 minutes.

Servings

Ingredients

  • 1 kg ripe tomatoes, halved
  • 1 onion, roughly chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, peeled
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 750 ml vegetable stock
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • Salt and black pepper
  • A handful of basil leaves, to serve

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 200 °C. Chop the onion and peel the garlic while it warms up, about 10 minutes.
  2. Roast the tomatoes, onion and garlic with the olive oil for 25 minutes, until the skins blister.
  3. Tip everything into a pot with the stock and simmer for 10 minutes.
  4. Blend until smooth, then season with salt, pepper and the sugar. Tear over the basil to serve.
